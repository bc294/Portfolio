Featured Projects



Barred Owl Habitat Expansion and its Impact on Northern Spotted Owls

This project examined the predicted habitat expansion of the Barred Owl ( Strix varia) and how that, along with climate change, will impact the habitat of the endangered Northern Spotted Owl ( Strix occidentalis caurina). 

  • Goal: Increase awareness of situation
  • Tools: ArcGIS Pro, MaxEnt
  • Skills: Habitat prediction

Employment relevance: Demonstrates wildlife analysis and species habitat prediction skills.



Modeling Habitat of the O'ahu 'Amakihi ( Chlorodrepanis flava)

This poster modeled the habitat of the near threatened O'ahu 'Amakihi using known observations and habitat preferences.

  • Goal: Model likely habitat of species
  • Tools: ArcGIS Pro
  • Skills: Spatial analysis tools, cartographic communication

Employment relevance: Shows comptenecy in using foundational spatial analysis tools to create applicable result.



Kinderhook Creek Watershed

This report modeled and analyzed the watershed of the Kinderhook Creek in the upper Hudson Valley of New York.

  • Goal: Model under-researched Kinderhook Creek watershed
  • Tools: ArcGIS Pro
  • Skills: ArcHydrology, spatial analysis

Employment relevance: Demonstrates knowledge and experience in using ArcHydrology tools in ArcGIS Pro.

 



Ranges and Characteristics of Families Hominidae and Hylobatidae

This project determined the ranges of families Hominidae and Hylobatidae as well as identified key characteristics of each family. 

  • Goal: Compare ranges and characteristics of families
  • Tools: ArcGIS Pro
  • Skills: Species range modeling

Employment relevance: Demonstrates range modeling and cartographic communication skills.
